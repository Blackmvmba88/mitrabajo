# BLACKMAMBA / Engineering manifesto

Intelligence without boundaries is not the goal. Intelligence with explicit, verifiable boundaries is.

1. Local-first when privacy, resilience or latency justify it.
2. Frontier models when measured task requirements justify them.
3. Authority is explicit, least-privilege and revocable.
4. Model output is evidence to evaluate, never authority to obey.
5. Fail closed on policy uncertainty; preserve human oversight.
6. Measure before optimizing. Publish failures alongside successes.
7. Prefer interoperable modules over unnecessary infrastructure.
8. Defense, authorization and responsible disclosure are non-negotiable.

## Victory conditions
A stranger can reproduce the demonstration, inspect its trust boundaries, challenge its conclusions and understand its limitations.

No theater. No invented metrics. Build the evidence.
